The Decoria Edit

Quiet Pieces with a Past

Every Decoria piece is chosen the way a curator chooses — for line, material and the quiet authority of good Nordic craft. From Hans Wegner's rope-and-oak Flag Halyard chair to Poul Henningsen's sculptural Artichoke pendant, the collection spans seven decades of Scandinavian design.

We photograph every piece in-house and describe it honestly, condition notes and all — so what arrives at your door is exactly what you fell for.
